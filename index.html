Base: The learnings from why the winners worked -- not the assets themselves. Explore freely. New copy, new visual concepts, new hooks. Hotel-quality sleep positioning must anchor every asset.

Layer 2 IS this
Entirely new concept -- e.g. hotel receipt visual comparing room rate vs $69.90
New format -- e.g. a list-style creative instead of headline + product shot

Track Breakdown -- 500 Assets
Track 01
Identity / Awareness
100 assets -- No price -- 40 Layer 1 / 60 Layer 2
Stop the scroll for cold audiences. Lead with a relatable moment or feeling, not a product claim. Make the reader feel seen before the product appears.
Example Headlines
"I'm at the age where I finally stopped settling for bad pillows"
"I'm at the age where good sleep is non-negotiable"
"Why does my bed never feel as good as a hotel bed"
"Things I no longer cheap out on: my pillow"
"Nobody warned me how much a good pillow changes everything"
Warm editorial bedroom scenes. Soft morning light. Aspirational but attainable. Minimal text overlay -- let the image carry the emotion. No price anywhere.
Track 02
Price / Value
100 assets -- 60 with price / 40 implied -- 40 Layer 1 / 60 Layer 2
Resolve the price objection for cold audiences. Position $69.90 as the smart obvious choice vs hotel markup.
With Price -- 60 assets
"Hotel pillows cost $200+. Yours is $69.90"
"The exact pillow from 5-star hotels. Not the 5-star price. $69.90"
"Hotel-quality sleep starts at $69.90"
"Four Seasons uses these. You can own them for $69.90"
"Stop renting hotel sleep. Own it for $69.90"
Implied Value -- 40 assets
"No hotel markup. Ever."
"The exact pillow luxury hotels use -- without the hotel bill"
"Stop paying hotel prices for hotel-quality sleep"
"Same pillow. A fraction of the price"
"Why are you still paying the hotel tax on your sleep"
CTA direction: "Shop Now" / "Get Yours Today" -- no price in button copy.
Track 03
Social Proof
100 assets -- No price -- 40 Layer 1 / 60 Layer 2
Let real customer language close the sale. Raw review copy builds trust faster than brand claims. Do not over-polish the language.
Review Angles -- 5 examples
"Feels like expensive hotel pillows without the high price"
"I actually look forward to going to bed now"
"Best pillows I've ever owned. I'll never buy another brand"
"So soft yet firm -- exactly what I needed"
"Worth every single penny. My only regret is not buying sooner"
Review card overlaid on product or lifestyle image. 5-star rating visible. "Verified Buyer" label present. Warm trustworthy aesthetic -- not clinical.
Track 04
Product Education
100 assets -- Selective price -- 40 Layer 1 / 60 Layer 2
Answer the "is down alternative as good as real down?" objection. Position as the smart, modern choice -- not a compromise.
Example Headlines
"Hypoallergenic. Hotel-quality. $69.90"
"Everything you love about hotel pillows. Nothing you don't"
"Down Alternative: softer, cleaner, no allergens"
"RDS certified. 794K+ happy sleepers"
"Why 794,000 people switched to FluffCo"
Split comparison layouts, feature callout bubbles, badge and icon grids. Reference Asset 2 and Asset 3 as visual style benchmarks for this track.
Track 05
Lifestyle / Aspiration
100 assets -- No price -- 40 Layer 1 / 60 Layer 2
Sell the feeling, not the product. Target people upgrading their home and sleep experience. Outcome-focused -- what does life feel like after buying this pillow. No price anywhere in this track.
Example Headlines
"Hotel-quality sleep. Every single night"
"The last pillow upgrade you'll ever need"
"Wake up feeling like you stayed at a 5-star hotel"
"What if every morning felt like a hotel checkout morning"
"Your bed should feel this good. Now it can."
Visual Direction
Morning routines, luxurious unmade beds, stacked pillows in warm-lit rooms. Lifestyle over product. Minimal text overlay -- let the visual sell the feeling. Warm cream and blush color temperature preferred. No price, no CTA button needed in this track.

Standing Instruction for Blitzads

Do not produce 500 assets as visual templates with swapped copy. Each track must feel visually distinct from the others. Each layer must feel structurally distinct from the other.

Tracks 1 and 5: editorial, organic, emotional   Tracks 2 and 4: direct-response, clear, conversion-focused   Track 3: human, authentic, review-native

If a Layer 2 asset could pass as a Layer 1 variation, it is not Layer 2. Regenerate.

If there are questions on visual direction, the 9 reference assets are the brand benchmark. Match their tone, palette, and quality standard -- then go beyond them.
